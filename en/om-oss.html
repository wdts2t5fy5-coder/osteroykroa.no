About Osterøykroa

Where good folk meet – since 1997.

Our story

Osterøykroa sits in the heart of Osterøy and has been a local meeting place since 1997. We serve home cooking and grilled food, offer catering and host warm evenings with live music – and quite possibly the best burger in Western Norway.

We are best known for our burger meals, which come in several sizes – from a good 100 g one up to the hearty 500 g Jafs Burger. We also serve freshly baked pizza, sizzling platters, fish & chips and other home cooking, with raspeballer, the local potato dumplings, every Thursday.

The atmosphere

Everyone is welcome at Osterøykroa – whether you drop in for a quick burger, bring the family for a platter, or stay for an evening of live music. We also cater for parties and events.

The charm of the place

Old Ringnes signs, a coin-operated slot machine, a vintage shop scale and pinball in "Skottegaten" – there is plenty to discover here.

Come and visit

You will find us at Lonevågen 2, in the middle of Lonevåg on Osterøy – do drop in.
