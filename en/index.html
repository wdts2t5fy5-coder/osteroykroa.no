A taste of the menu

We are best known for our burgers, but we also serve pizza, sizzling platters and fish & chips. On Thursdays we serve raspeballer, the local potato dumplings.

Burger meals

Our burger meals come in several sizes, from 100 g up to the hearty 500 g version – served with a drink and fries.

Freshly baked pizza

15 different pizzas, or build your own. Gluten-free options too.

Raspeballer platter

Beef, chicken, shaved beef, kebab, schnitzel or fish & chips – all served with a fresh salad.

About Osterøykroa

Osterøykroa sits in the heart of Osterøy and has been a local meeting place since 1997. We serve home cooking and grilled food, offer catering and host warm evenings with live music – and quite possibly the best burger in Western Norway.
