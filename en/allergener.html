Fries
May contain traces of gluten

Most dishes can be made gluten-free. They are still listed with gluten, because traces of gluten come from the preparation of the fries. We also offer a gluten-free burger bun as an option – get in touch and we will find something that works.

Our dishes are prepared in the same kitchen, so we cannot guarantee that a dish is entirely free from traces of other allergens. If you have an allergy we should know about, tell us at the counter or call +47 56 19 34 34, and we will work it out together.
