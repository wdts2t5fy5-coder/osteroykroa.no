What is logged

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like all web servers, they log IP address, time, which page was requested and browser type – in order to deliver the pages and protect against abuse. The logs are used for operations and security, not for marketing, and they are deleted automatically after a short period.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in operating and securing the website (GDPR article 6(1)(f)).

The map from Google

The map on our contact page does not load on its own. If you press “Click to load map”, Google receives your IP address, information about your browser and device, and which page you came from. Google may store information in your browser and transfer the data outside the EEA – see Google's privacy policy. If you would rather avoid it, use the “Open in Google Maps instead” link.

Legal basis: your consent (article 6(1)(a)). We do not store your consent – it applies to that one page view, so the next time you open the page the map is unloaded again.

Your rights

There is little to find, but you have the right to see whatever does exist about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Contact us at roydy@icloud.com. You may also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority.
